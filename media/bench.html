Earthen Bench in Crete

For 5th and last year in architecture I got into Erasmus Mobility, going to Chaniá, Crete, Greece. For the assignment of constructions with natural materials (Ειδικές κατασκευές με φυσικά υλικά), a small and quite challenging bench was planned and made entirelly out of adobe bricks, hugging an olive tree, with the intent of understanding how the material works, My role in this project was construction of stone wall/bench close to the tree and part of the earthen bench, the solar and usable orientation, modeling and 3d scans.
